We presented the first GPU port of LESGO, a pseudo-spectral wind-farm
large-eddy simulation solver comprising nearly 70{,}000 lines of
Fortran code. We demonstrated that porting such a legacy codebase
requires a fine-grained data-management strategy. We classified every
host--device coupling point in LESGO and presented four treatments
that systematically decide compute offloading and data movement. In addition, we presented three
optimizations, including chunk pipelining across serial process chains,
batching many small work items, and CPU--GPU heterogeneous overlap.
We believe these
optimizations extend beyond LESGO to other multiphysics solvers. 
On a production-scale benchmark, our GPU implementation delivers a
40$\times$ to 90$\times$ speedup over the CPU-only version. 
We expect this to shorten a full production campaign of a 
60-turbine wind farm from months to days.
